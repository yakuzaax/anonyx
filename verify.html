You are right to distrust a tool that handles videos of people. No need to believe us: everything can be checked.

What Anonyx does

  • On your device. The video is analysed in the browser. It is sent nowhere.
  • A solid black square. Painted on the frame before it is saved again: the face is not blurred or covered, it is no longer in the file. An optional blur exists, but it keeps colours and shape, so the black square stays the default.
  • No traces. Location, date and phone model, often kept in the file, are removed.
  • No account. No ads, no analytics. Like any site, the host (Cloudflare) sees who connects to anonyx.pages.dev, never the videos.

If you can code

  • Public code. Free licence: github.com/yakuzaax/anonyx. The site serves the same readable files, nothing hidden.
  • No trace of the face. test/test-no-trace.mjs takes two videos identical except for the faces and applies the same masks (black square or emoji): the output files are byte-for-byte identical. No watermark can lead back to the face.
  • Nothing sent. The page forbids the browser from connecting to any site other than anonyx.pages.dev.
  • Original libraries. ONNX Runtime and Mediabunny: test/verify-vendor.sh checks they match the official releases. The detection model is YuNet, retrained to see better at night: the recipe is public.

What Anonyx does not do

No tool finds every face: a profile, a partly hidden or very small face can be missed. Tap the image to add a mask by hand.

The square hides the face, not clothes, tattoos, gait or place. Sound is removed by default. The “voice” option changes pitch and tone: it makes recognition harder, not impossible.
